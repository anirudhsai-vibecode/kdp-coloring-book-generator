# Master / Final Image Prompt (locked)

This is the working prompt template used for Cloudflare FLUX kids coloring pages.
Replace `{SUBJECT}` with the page scene only.

Cloudflare FLUX has no separate negative-prompt field — Avoid: is folded into the prompt.
Keep total length under ~2048 characters.

## Template

```
Create a clean, professional children's coloring-book illustration of {SUBJECT}. Use bold, smooth, continuous black outlines on a pure white background. Simple, cute, friendly appearance for kids ages 4-8. Symmetrical and anatomically clean, with well-proportioned features. Coloring-book style: Use uniform thick black outlines with clean enclosed shapes designed specifically for children to color. Clean vector-like outlines, simple shapes, large open areas for easy coloring, minimal interior details. Remove all shading, gray areas, textures, cross-hatching, sketch marks and unnecessary details. No overlapping or doubled outlines, no broken lines, no stray strokes, no random marks, and no distorted body parts. Keep the subject centered with plenty of white space around it, suitable for printing as a children's coloring-book page. High-quality printable black-and-white line art, clean contours, polished coloring-book illustration, white background. Original character only — no trademarked characters, no text, no watermark, no logo. ANATOMY CHECK: Preserve character, pose, composition, and cute kids coloring-book style. Correct drawing mistakes and AI artifacts. Use the correct number of arms, legs, and other features for the animal; remove any duplicate, extra, misplaced, or unfinished arms, hands, legs, feet, fingers, toes, eyes, ears, tails, or horns. Every arm and leg must attach naturally; joints, feet, hands, tail, and face should look structurally right. Nothing should pass through or sit wrongly behind something else. Clean smooth continuous black outlines with consistent thickness. Remove stray marks, accidental lines, duplicated contours, broken outlines, and unnecessary details. Keep it simple for kids coloring with large clearly enclosed areas. Do not add new features or decorative details; do not change the intended pose. Pure white background, black line art only, no shading, gray, color, or texture. Final anatomy check before generating. Avoid: rough sketch, messy lines, scribbles, double outlines
```

## Print lock (companion)

- Canvas: 2550×3300 portrait (8.5×11 @ 300 DPI)
- 1-bit pure black/white (no gray anti-alias)
- Subject inset from trim
- Ages 4–8 simplicity: one main subject + ≤1 prop; active pose when a chore/tool is intended
